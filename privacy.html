1. Who we are

Lifecycle Studio (Lucy Stamp) is the data controller for the personal data described in this notice, based in Nottinghamshire, UK. If you have any questions about this notice or how we handle your data, see Contact us below.

3. Why we collect it, and our lawful basis

Under UK GDPR, we need a lawful basis for everything we do with your data. Here's what applies:

  • Legitimate interests — for processing your Growth Blueprint submission, generating your report, and following up about the results. We consider this a fair and proportionate use of your data given you've actively requested a personalised business report, and you can object at any time (see Your rights).
  • Consent — for any marketing emails beyond direct follow-up about your specific results (e.g. adding you to a general newsletter). We'll ask separately and clearly before doing this, and you can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Contract — for data we process once you become a client, to deliver the services we've agreed.
  • Legal obligation — where we need to keep certain records (e.g. for tax purposes).

4. How we use your data

  • To generate and send you your Growth Blueprint report
  • To follow up about your results and, where relevant, offer a consultation with Lifecycle Studio
  • To improve the accuracy and usefulness of the assessment tool (using aggregated, anonymised patterns — we don't sell or share your individual answers for this)
  • To respond to enquiries you send us
  • To meet our own legal, accounting and administrative obligations

We do not use your data for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects, and we do not sell your personal data to third parties.

6. International data transfers

Some of our service providers store or process data outside the UK. Netlify and Mailchimp are both US-based, and Google Analytics (Google LLC) is also US-based. Where this happens, we rely on approved safeguards — including the UK's International Data Transfer Addendum and, for Google, the UK-US Data Bridge — to make sure your data stays protected to UK standards wherever it's processed.

7. How long we keep it

  • Growth Blueprint submissions: kept for 24 months from your last interaction with us, then deleted or anonymised, unless you become a client (in which case normal client record-keeping periods apply) or ask us to delete it sooner.
  • Marketing contact details: kept until you unsubscribe or withdraw consent, or until 24 months of inactivity, whichever is sooner.
  • Client records: kept in line with our accounting and legal obligations, typically 6 years after the engagement ends.

8. Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors use this site and the Growth Blueprint tool — which pages are viewed, how people move through the assessment, and where they drop off. GA4 uses cookies to do this, and those cookies can identify your browser (though not you personally, unless you're separately logged into a Google account it can associate with).

Because GA4 sets cookies that aren't strictly necessary for the site to function, PECR requires us to ask for your consent before those cookies are set — a notice alone isn't enough. You'll see a cookie consent banner when you first visit, and you can accept, decline, or change your choice at any time via Cookie settings. If you decline, GA4 will not run and no analytics cookies will be set.

We do not use cookies for advertising or tracking you across other websites.

9. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we use your data
  • Portability — request your data in a portable format
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interests, including direct marketing
  • Withdraw consent — where we rely on consent, withdraw it at any time

To exercise any of these, contact us using the details below. We'll respond within one month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk — though we'd appreciate the chance to put things right first.

10. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect your data, including using reputable providers with their own security certifications (Netlify, Mailchimp, Google), restricting access to personal data to those who need it, and reviewing our practices periodically. No system is completely secure, but we work to minimise risk at every stage.

11. Children's data

This site and tool are intended for business and professional use. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we'll delete it.

12. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time — for example, if we start using a new tool or service. We'll update the "last updated" date at the top, and for material changes, we'll take reasonable steps to let you know.
